Paper: Double Q-learning (van Hasselt, 2010)
Venue: NeurIPS 2010

What to reproduce
Only the roulette experiment (Section 4.1): Q-learning and Double Q-learning, with the same learning-rate
schedules, trial counts and number of runs as the paper. The grid world experiment (Section 4.2) is out of
scope; do not extract claims from it.

Compute budget
- No GPU. CPU only.
- Hardware: laptop CPU
- If the scope cannot fit the budget, reduce it, and say what you cut and why.

Report
- The measured result for each claim of this experiment, with run IDs.
- A verdict: reproduced, partially reproduced, not reproduced, or could not test, with the reason.
- Every assumption or deviation from the paper (seeds, training length, details the paper does not state).

Rules
- Do not use the authors' code or third-party reproductions of this paper.
- Standard libraries (PyTorch, JAX, NumPy, SciPy, torchvision models, public datasets) are fine.
